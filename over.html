Privacy

  • No account needed.
  • Your location is only used when you allow it.
  • GPX routes stay on your device.
  • When you open a place, nook asks Wikimedia Commons, Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap for a nearby photo, the elevation and the nearest town. Only the place's coordinates are sent.
  • We count successful GPX imports without cookies or route data, and keep the counts for 90 days.
  • Feedback is stored with technical details.
