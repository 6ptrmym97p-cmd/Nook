About Nook

Sleep spots for people on the move.

Nook helps bikepackers and touring cyclists find a feasible place to sleep along a route. The map combines official places, guest networks, public map data, campsites and private GPX files.

Who it’s for

On the road with bicycle and tent

Nook is intended for multi-day cycling trips. You can search for shelters, bivouac sites, free tent areas, guest gardens, cycling accommodation addresses, campsites and your own waypoints.

Data

What stays in your browser

GPX routes, custom points and preferences are saved locally or for the session only. Other visitors do not see that data. Guest network contact information remains with the original platform.

Privacy

No account; routes on your device

Nook does not require an account. Your location will only be used with permission. GPX routes remain in your browser. If you send feedback, your message and the associated technical data will be stored.

We count successful GPX imports without cookies or route data. These counts are kept for 90 days.
